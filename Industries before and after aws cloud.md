### **How Industries Worked Before AWS vs. After AWS**  

Before AWS, companies had to **set up and maintain their own physical servers**. This led to **high costs, slow deployment, and scalability issues**. AWS revolutionized the industry by providing **on-demand cloud computing**, allowing businesses to scale flexibly and reduce operational costs.  

---

### **Before AWS (Traditional IT Infrastructure)**
🔹 **Setup**: Companies had to buy expensive physical servers, networking devices, and storage.  
🔹 **Maintenance**: They needed IT teams to manage hardware, security, backups, and scaling.  
🔹 **Scalability**: Businesses had to estimate their needs in advance, leading to over-provisioning or shortages.  
🔹 **Deployment Speed**: Setting up a new application could take weeks or months.  
🔹 **Disaster Recovery**: Companies had to maintain backup servers at different locations.  

**Example: Netflix Before AWS**  
- Before AWS, Netflix had to maintain its own data centers.  
- A sudden increase in traffic (e.g., when a new show launched) caused **server overloads and streaming failures**.  
- Scaling up required buying new servers, which took months.  

---

### **After AWS (Cloud Computing Era)**
🔹 **On-Demand Resources**: Companies rent compute power, storage, and databases as needed.  
🔹 **Cost-Efficiency**: No upfront investment in hardware, pay only for what you use.  
🔹 **Instant Scalability**: Businesses scale up/down in minutes based on demand.  
🔹 **Faster Deployment**: Applications can be launched in **minutes instead of months**.  
🔹 **Automated Backups & Security**: AWS manages disaster recovery, security, and compliance.  

**Example: Netflix After AWS**  
- Moved entirely to AWS in 2016.  
- Uses **EC2** to handle millions of video streams globally.  
- Uses **S3** for storing all media files securely.  
- Uses **AWS Auto Scaling** to handle peak traffic during new releases.  
- Result: **No downtime, instant scalability, and lower costs**.  

---

### **Other Real-Life Examples of AWS Transformation**
1️⃣ **Airbnb**  
   - Before AWS: Had to maintain their own infrastructure, leading to slow feature releases.  
   - After AWS: Uses **EC2, RDS, and DynamoDB** to handle millions of bookings daily with auto-scaling.  

2️⃣ **NASA**  
   - Before AWS: Data stored in physical locations, making global access difficult.  
   - After AWS: Uses **AWS S3** to store and distribute space images to the public.  

3️⃣ **Flipkart (Indian E-commerce)**  
   - Before AWS: Faced **server crashes** during the Big Billion Days sale.  
   - After AWS: Uses **AWS Auto Scaling** to handle high traffic automatically.
