### 1   **Object Storage – Amazon S3** *(Highly scalable and durable)*  

Hey there! I’m **Amazon S3**, your **go-to object storage** solution in AWS. Need a place to store massive amounts of **unstructured data** like images, videos, backups, and logs? I’ve got you covered!

- **Cost Optimization** – Choose from storage classes:

- **Integration** – Works seamlessly with AWS services like **EC2, Lambda, Athena, Redshift, and CloudFront**.

### 2   **Block Storage – Amazon EBS** *(High-performance for EC2 instances)*  

Hey there! I’m **Amazon EBS (Elastic Block Store)**, your **high-performance, low-latency storage solution** built for **Amazon EC2** instances. If you need **persistent, fast, and flexible block storage**, I’m the one for you!  

### **What I Offer:**  
- **High Performance** – Supports high IOPS & throughput for demanding applications.  
- **Persistence** – Data remains intact even if your EC2 instance stops or restarts.  
- **Scalability** – Easily scale storage up or down as per your needs.  
- **Backup & Recovery** – Snapshots for backups, disaster recovery, and data cloning.  
- **Security** – Encryption, IAM controls, and compliance-ready features.  
- **Multiple Volume Types** *(Choose based on performance & cost)*:  
  - **General Purpose SSD (gp3, gp2)** – Balanced cost-performance for most workloads.  
  - **Provisioned IOPS SSD (io2, io1)** – Ultra-high performance for databases & critical apps.  
  - **Throughput Optimized HDD (st1)** – Cost-efficient for big data & streaming.  
  - **Cold HDD (sc1)** – Lowest-cost storage for rarely accessed data.  

I integrate seamlessly with **EC2**, making sure your applications run **fast, reliably, and securely**.
